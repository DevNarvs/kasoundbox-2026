## How to read this document

- **What an edge case is.** An unusual situation: two people acting at once, a deadline that has just passed, a mistake, or a system problem. Each row says what the system does, and which rule it relies on.
- **This document makes no new rules.** Every row traces back to spec v0.2. The "Relies on" column names the owning doc and its ID, and the spec section the row comes from. If a row here and its owning doc ever disagree, the owning doc is right, and this row must be corrected.
- **Columns.**
  - **Situation:** what happens.
  - **What the system does:** the expected behavior. *Examples* use made-up names, prices, and slot times. The real slot times and on-site hours are open item O-02; the real prices, stock, sets, and deposit amount are O-03.
  - **Relies on:** the rule (BR), requirement (FR, NFR), decision (D), or owning doc behind the behavior, and the spec section.
  - **Verified by:** where the test for this row will live. The test cases (TC-xx) will be written in the test plan (planned for batch 4: `06-delivery/test-plan.md`).
- **Labels.** **[C]** confirmed decision · **[P]** proposed, accepted with the spec approval but may change · **[U]** unverified fact, to be checked in the week-1 test or before launch · **[O-nn]** open item, listed in the [decision log](../00-project/decision-log.md). A behavior with no label is part of the approved design (spec v0.2, approved on 2026-10-21, D-23).
- **Admins.** "An admin" means either admin account. "The Super Admin" is the developer. "The Admin" is the business owner. Who may do each action is defined only in [roles-and-permissions](roles-and-permissions.md).
- **Time and money.** Every date and time is Manila time (Asia/Manila). Every amount is in Philippine pesos (₱).
- **Words.** Terms such as *hold*, *live claim*, *block*, *service start*, and *closure date* are explained in the [glossary](../00-project/glossary.md).

## 3. Deadlines and expiry

The deadlines themselves are defined only in [reservation-lifecycle](../02-domain/reservation-lifecycle.md). In short: 72 hours to review, 24 hours to pay the deposit after approval, and never later than the service start **[C]** ([D-04](../00-project/decision-log.md)).

| ID | Situation | What the system does | Relies on | Verified by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EC-20 | Nobody reviews a request, and the event is less than 72 hours away. | The request's deadline is its service start, not submission + 72 hours. If no admin acts by then, it expires, its capacity is freed, and the expired email is queued. So no request is still waiting for review or a deposit once its service start has passed. *Example:* submitted Monday 21 December 2026, 10:00 AM, for a delivery slot starting Tuesday 22 December, 2:00 PM. Deadline: Tuesday 22 December, 2:00 PM. | [reservation-lifecycle](../02-domain/reservation-lifecycle.md) (deadlines) · [FR-SYS-01, FR-NTF-12](functional-requirements.md) · spec §7 | test-plan (batch 4) |
| EC-21 | An admin approves a request close to the event. | The deposit deadline is approval + 24 hours or the service start, whichever is earlier. *Example:* approved Wednesday 23 December 2026, 4:00 PM, for a slot starting Thursday 24 December, 10:00 AM. The deposit is due Thursday 24 December, 10:00 AM. | [reservation-lifecycle](../02-domain/reservation-lifecycle.md) (deadlines) · spec §7 | test-plan (batch 4) |
| EC-22 | A hold's deadline has passed, but the every-minute job has not yet marked it Expired. | It counts as expired everywhere at once. The calendar treats its set or room as free, the status page shows Expired, and admin actions treat it as expired. The job only tidies up. | EC-40 | A payment or refund is recorded long after the booking reached its final status. | The closure date is recomputed **[P]**, so the retention periods count from the later date. *Example:* Ben's booking is cancelled on Tuesday 22 December 2026, and he collects his ₱500 refund on Monday 4 January 2027. The closure date moves to 4 January 2027, so his contact details are blanked from Thursday 4 January 2029 instead of Saturday 23 December 2028 ([privacy-and-retention](privacy-and-retention.md) §6.3, Example B). | [privacy-and-retention](privacy-and-retention.md) (closure date) · [FR-ADM-19](functional-requirements.md) · spec §11 | test-plan (batch 4) |

## 15. Situations the spec does not fully answer

Spec v0.2 does not settle these. They are **not decided here**, and the rows above do not assume an answer. If the reviewers agree an answer is needed, add each one to the [decision log](../00-project/decision-log.md) as a new open item (O-12 onwards), then update the owning doc and the edge case named.

1. **A block over a date range, where one date already has a live booking (EC-18).** A range creates one block per date, and a block is refused when its target is taken (BR-08). Is the whole range refused, or only the date that clashes?
2. **Recreating an expired request whose service start has already passed (EC-52).** The new 72-hour deadline is capped at the service start, which would already be in the past. What should happen?
3. **A customer pays only part of the deposit (EC-34).** Only the exact deposit can be recorded. What do the admins do with the part payment?
4. **A deadline extended while expiry is paused for proof review (EC-25, EC-27).** If the proof is then rejected, "the original deadline applies again" (BR-12). Is that the deadline before or after the extension?
5. **The default balance after a waived deposit (EC-38, EC-44).** The balance defaults to "total minus deposit". When the deposit was waived and never paid, should the default be the full total?

These are already listed in [functional-requirements](functional-requirements.md) section 7, and are not repeated here: emails for a one-step staff booking (question 1, EC-41); the reminder and the deadline shown while expiry is paused (question 2, EC-27); recording a deposit after a void on a Confirmed booking (question 3, EC-33); how the customer gets a QR code reissued by an admin (question 4, EC-62); and whether "event not held" sends the cancelled email (question 7, EC-51).

## 16. Adding or changing an edge case

- **A new edge case** gets the next free number (EC-86 onwards). Numbers are never reused or renumbered.
- **An edge case only describes behavior.** If a new situation needs a new rule, the rule comes first: record it in the [decision log](../00-project/decision-log.md) (a new D-xx, or an O-xx while it is open), then in its owning doc. Only then is the row added here, linking to that rule.
- **A changed rule** changes the rows that rely on it. Follow "How to change a decision" in the decision log: update the log first, then every doc in that decision's "Docs affected", including this one.
- **A withdrawn edge case** stays in its table, marked "Withdrawn", with the date and the decision ID.
- **The frozen spec is never edited** ([D-23](../00-project/decision-log.md)).
